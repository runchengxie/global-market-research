## Design

Use an external sealed pre-acquisition intent binding the original protocol hash, unchanged core parameters, calendar-year episode dates and stopping rule. After bounded public acquisition, finalize source-bound manifests/protocols without changing those choices. All data remain retrospective; do not claim historical preregistration, independent upstream lineage, or unseen OOS.

Extend USMV history toward inception using the existing provider chart contract. Attempt the issuer's linked official fund-download workbook as corroboration. Preserve bytes/URL/times/hashes, inspect sheet/column semantics read-only. NAV, raw market close, adjusted close and performance indices cannot be substituted. Do not bypass browser challenges, log in or use credentials. If compatible independent adjusted-OHLC data are absent, state that gap; raw-close/dividend corroboration is partial and cannot certify a full replay or corporate-action/PIT semantics.

Reuse volatility_study.run and its pure arithmetic, not a second strategy engine. One earlier calibration episode, then nonoverlapping full calendar-year evaluation episodes plus a separately labeled partial final year. Core lookback/target/cap/cadence/fees/buffer stay identical to the original protocol. Every arm/fee/year remains retained; resets/entry/exit costs in each annual episode are explicit. This is not a continuous full-span return account. Annual boundaries are fixed independently of outcomes and are not named market regimes.

A configurable but pre-frozen stopping rule compares primary volatility_control to hindsight_exposure_matched only; that comparator remains nontradable and must meet exposure tolerance. At each specified positive fee, an episode passes when return and max drawdown are no worse within a frozen numerical tolerance and at least one improves beyond a frozen materiality tolerance. Require at least two-thirds of designated new full-year episodes at every decision fee. Exclude previously examined years and partial years from the decision denominator but display them. Report research-continue/retire under these inputs, not universal strategy invalidity or actual adoption. Incomplete planned results produce unknown, never deletion or pass. Independent-source adequacy is a separate reported gate.

Keep input contracts general and parameters private. New code should be a small sealed report aggregator/validation layer over existing studies, plus narrowly needed source extraction/corroboration. No copied risk book, broker/execution/leaf import, automatic targets, schedule or platform changes. Retain failed attempts and all original reports. English/zh-CN operations guidance stays aligned.
